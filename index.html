💡 Key Engineering Rule for Designers & Brand Owners:
A garment cut from rigid fabric (like a denim jacket) needs Positive Ease (+4 cm to +8 cm bigger than the body) so you can move. High-stretch athletic apparel requires Negative Ease (-8% to -18%) so the fabric hugs the curves and holds itself firmly against saddle friction.

🔬 The Science: Why Equestrian Leggings Cannot Be Cut 1:1 to Body Size

❌ Case A: Cutting 1:1 (71 cm Table Measure)

Result: Critical Functional Failure in Saddle.
If the waistband is cut flat at 35.5 cm (71 cm loop) to match the rider's exact waist:

  • Zero elastic tension holding the pants up.
  • The pants slide down instantly when mounting the horse.
  • Excess fabric folds and creates painful pressure creases between rider and saddle.
  • Full-seat silicone grip cannot grip properly because the fabric is sagging.

✅ Case B: Conbello Engineered Fit (33 cm Table Measure)

Result: Zero Defect Professional Riding Performance.
With our engineered -5.5 cm negative ease (33 cm flat 1/2 waist):

  • The 22% Spandex core activates moderate compressive force.
  • High anatomical double-layer waistband stays anchored over the iliac crest.
  • Fabric remains 100% squat-proof without over-sheering (300 GSM interlock density).
  • Silicone grip stays flat and firm against saddle leather.
