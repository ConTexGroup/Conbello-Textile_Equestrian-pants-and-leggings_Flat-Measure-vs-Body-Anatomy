# Conbello Textile - Equestrian Pants & Leggings Sizing Guide

An interactive engineering simulator and technical documentation comparing **Garment Flat Measure vs. Body Anatomy** for high-performance equestrian apparel[cite: 1].

🔗 **Live Simulator:** [Conbello Textile Equestrian Leggings Sizing Guide](https://contexgroup.github.io/Conbello-Textile_Equestrian-pants-and-leggings_Flat-Measure-vs-Body-Anatomy/)[cite: 1]

## Overview
In professional equestrian apparel manufacturing (specifically 300 GSM / 22% Spandex Interlock fabrics), understanding **Negative Ease** is crucial[cite: 1]. This simulator visualizes why an EU 38 Medium riding legging measures 33 cm flat on the cutting table, yet fits a 71 cm rider's waist securely in the saddle[cite: 1].

## Key Engineering Features
- **Interactive Sizing Simulator:** Real-time calculation of waist/hip stretch tension, flat measurements, and negative ease deltas across EU sizes (34 to 42)[cite: 1].
- **Anatomical vs. Flat Measure Analysis:** Explains why 1:1 cutting fails in the saddle (causing slippage and pressure creases) versus engineered compression[cite: 1].
- **Standard EU Size Reference Table:** Comprehensive data mapping rider body dimensions against cutting-table specifications[cite: 1].
