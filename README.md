## 📌 Why Flat Measure vs. Body Anatomy Matters
In professional **riding leggings and breeches production**, fabric elastane ratios and high-performance compression require precise measurement adjustments[cite: 1]. 

- **Flat Measure:** The dimensions of the equestrian pants when laid completely flat on a table[cite: 1].
- **Body Anatomy:** The actual physical measurements of the rider's body[cite: 1].
- **The Formula:** Understanding the stretch percentage based on high-quality fabrics like Polyamide, Spandex, and custom knitted blends[cite: 1].
